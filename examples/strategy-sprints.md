# We ran it on ourselves first

Strategy Sprints helps owners of B2B companies take control of the sales conversation. We scored our own business on 7 October 2026, honestly.

| # | Question | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Creates value | 2 | We sell growth (more deals, bigger deals, a team that closes without the owner), not savings. Risk we watch: "AI sales processes" sliding into "replace your SDR", which puts us next to cheap AI SDR tools. |
| Q2 | Worth paying for | 1 | Our core buyer has an expensive problem (missing the annual target, a sales hire who flops). But our cheapest doors (a $49 community, a $199 workshop) set a low price anchor. |
| Q3 | Root drive | 2 | The owner's fear is named in every call: what happens if the pipeline looks the same in 3 months? |
| Q4 | Champion story | 1 | Selling to the owner means no boss to convince, which keeps deals short. But we hadn't written the story the owner tells the people *they* answer to (partner, team, the new sales hire). |
| Q5 | Forcing function | 2 | We only target owners hiring salespeople right now: the money is committed and the clock is running. |
| Q6 | Own data | 1 | 2,400+ founders, weekly scorecards, call recordings: data nobody else has. Not used yet. |
| Q7 | Network | 1 | Small coaching groups where owners see each other. No pooled benchmark yet. |

**10/14, PREMIUM-READY, no flags.**

**What we changed:**
- Lead every owner conversation with the cost of the problem, not the price of our cheapest door.
- Wrote the champion's story: "I installed the sales process before I hired, so the new hire performs from week one."
- Queued a private benchmark (win rate, deal size and cycle length by sales step, across owners like you): the data stack and the network in one.

**The one move:** add "days from first call to signature" to every deal in our CRM, this week.
